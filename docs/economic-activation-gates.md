# Economic activation gates

This is an engineering checklist, not legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice.

Economically valuable CARROT remains disabled until the project completes external review appropriate to the jurisdictions and distribution channels involved.

Required gates include protocol/security review; adversarial public-testnet evidence across independent operators; founder/treasury custody and recovery drills; tax/accounting analysis; applicable securities/commodities and money-transmission analysis; contest/prize-law review for CARROT-linked competitions; Apple App Store and Google Play policy review; privacy/data-protection review; consumer disclosures and terms; sanctions/AML analysis where applicable; and incident-response/key-compromise procedures.

Technical readiness alone is not authorization to activate a valuable token.
