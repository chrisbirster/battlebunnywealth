# Economy

## Principle: separate the game economy from the network economy

Battle Bunny Wealth has two intentionally separate economic systems:

```text
SEASONAL GAME ECONOMY
businesses -> Bunny Bucks -> season standings -> cosmetics/collection/progression

NETWORK ECONOMY
Proof of Play -> authority/eligibility -> CARROT issuance/rewards
```

They can interact at carefully defined boundaries, but they must never collapse into one currency.

## Seasonal game economy

### Bunny Bucks

Bunny Bucks are the primary idle-business currency.

- generated by player-owned bunny businesses
- used to upgrade and optimize those businesses
- measured heavily within a season
- can be turned in at season end for persistent game rewards
- can reset or partially reset between seasons
- have no fixed global supply
- are not CARROT
- are not a blockchain asset by default

The purpose of Bunny Bucks is to make the idle optimization game fun. Inflation is acceptable because seasons create a reset boundary.

### Other game resources

Candidate resources include:

- Scrap — crafting/collection resource.
- Medals — permanent rank/progression/trophy resource.
- Blueprints — unlock cosmetic or collection options.
- Decorations — persistent warren customization.

The word "carrot" should not be reused as a generic premium game currency because **CARROT is the network coin**.

## Season turn-in

At the end of a season, players convert or spend seasonal wealth on persistent rewards.

Allowed directions include:

- bunny cosmetics
- uniforms/headgear
- bomb skins
- explosion effects
- emotes/victory poses
- profile banners/titles
- medals/trophies
- base decorations
- story/collection unlocks
- cosmetic arena themes

Seasonal wealth must **not** purchase ranked combat power.

A player who generated 1000x more Bunny Bucks than another player still enters a ranked Warren Wars match with the same starting combat state.

## Ranked Warren Wars economy rule

No external wallet or progression balance changes a player's starting ranked statistics.

CARROT, Bunny Bucks, account age, season rank, cosmetics, purchases, and Proof-of-Play authority cannot grant:

- extra starting bombs
- larger starting blast radius
- faster movement
- extra health/lives
- shorter fuse timers
- stronger damage
- privileged spawn locations
- any equivalent competitive advantage

Gameplay-affecting powerups are acquired inside the match under the same rules for every participant.

The design rule is:

> You can buy style. You can earn status. You cannot buy skill.

## CARROT

**CARROT** is the planned native network coin for the Proof-of-Play blockchain layer.

Current design intent:

- fixed maximum supply, conceptually similar to Bitcoin scarcity
- no unlimited inflation
- a declining issuance schedule should be researched, potentially using eras/halving-like reductions
- Proof-of-Play participation is the primary candidate mechanism for distributing network issuance
- CARROT is distinct from seasonal game money
- the exact maximum supply, emission curve, block/epoch reward, fees, and divisibility are not yet finalized

The protocol must define supply and issuance deterministically before any economically valuable public network launches.

## Founder/admin allocation

The current product decision is that **20% of the fixed CARROT supply is reserved for the founder/admin allocation**.

The remaining 80% must be explicitly allocated before launch among categories such as:

- Proof-of-Play network issuance
- ecosystem/game rewards
- community treasury
- liquidity/network bootstrapping

Those percentages are not yet finalized.

The 20% founder/admin allocation should be transparent in genesis/tokenomics documentation. A vesting or lock schedule is strongly preferred so the allocation cannot unexpectedly enter circulation at once, but the exact vesting schedule remains TBD.

This is an allocation of the fixed supply, **not a permanent 20% tax on every transaction or player reward** unless a future protocol decision explicitly changes that model.

## CARROT use cases

CARROT should not be required to enjoy the basic idle game or to obtain ranked combat power.

Candidate network/game uses include:

- protocol fees
- Proof-of-Play validator/committee rewards
- governance where appropriate
- marketplace settlement
- community-created content publishing bonds
- anti-spam deposits
- tournament/event infrastructure where legally and product-wise appropriate
- cosmetic/collectible marketplace activity

Any use involving real-money entry fees, pooled prizes, wagering-like mechanics, token sales, or cash-equivalent rewards requires dedicated legal, tax, app-store, and product review before implementation.

## Issuance philosophy

Bitcoin proves scarce issuance through Proof of Work. Battle Bunny Wealth is researching whether a fixed-supply asset can instead distribute issuance through qualified Proof-of-Play participation.

Conceptually:

```text
Bitcoin
fixed supply + declining issuance + Proof of Work

Battle Bunny Wealth
fixed CARROT supply + declining issuance + Proof of Play
```

The analogy is about supply discipline, not a claim that Proof of Play currently has Bitcoin-level security.

## Non-goals

- Do not tokenize every inventory item.
- Do not make idle-game numbers directly redeemable one-for-one for CARROT.
- Do not promise passive financial returns for leaving the app open.
- Do not let wallet size determine Warren Wars outcomes.
- Do not design the game around speculative token price.
